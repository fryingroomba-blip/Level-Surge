IN THE ROOM

The red thing, the door, and the teeth. These are frames from play. The wide one is the corridor with spikes above and below. The others are Nullscape and Frostbite.

THE DEAL

You are a small red thing with horns. The exit is a gold-and-red door. Touch the real one and the room is cleared. That is the only honest rule. Everything else is a delay.

One room at a time

Walk, jump, and reach the door. Clear the room, then the next. Finish every room in a dimension and the next world wakes up.

The room is watching

Traps wait on what you do: walk too far, jump too soon, stand still, sprint, or trust the wrong door. Some tells are dust. Some are teeth.

Death is a restart

You die, the game roasts you, and you respawn in the same room. The death counter stays until you start a new run of that world.

THE HUD

The top bar is the only honest narrator, and even it lies about the trap count until something fires.

Dimension

Something like D1 NULLSCAPE. Each world has its own sky, weather, and music.

Room

A number, a name, and pips for how far you are through this world.

????

The trap counter. It stays a question until traps actually go off.

Timer and deaths

How long this run has lasted, and how many times the room has won.

SURGE combo

Traps you survive in a short window stack a combo. Die and it drops.

Shards

Gold coins that do not explode. Some rooms make greed fatal instead.

Clearing a whole world prints a SURGE RATING. It is a huge number on purpose. It is theater, saved beside your best deaths and time for that dimension.

NULLSCAPE

Dimension 1 is handmade. Sixteen rooms, ten traps each. The void learned your name. Later worlds are generated from a seed, so they stay the same every time you enter them, but they are not these rooms.

TWENTY WORLDS

Rooms equal 15 plus the dimension number, so Nullscape has 16 and Last Light has 35. Trap count starts at 10 and climbs by 2, then 3, then 2, then 3. From Dimension 2 on, the last room is a core and carries four extra traps. Clear a world to unlock the next. Progress lives in this browser under the save name level-surge-v3.

WHAT A ROOM IS MADE OF

Each room is a tile grid plus a list of traps. A trap waits for a condition, then does something. A delay means it hits a beat late. A quiet trap, like dust or a fake beam, does not count toward your combo.

It waits for you

Walk past a point, jump, jump too early, land, stand still, or touch the fake door.

Then it answers

A hole, spikes, a late saw, a laser, flipped controls, a moved door, a shove, a falling brick.

Some of it is a lie

Dust and a glow can mean nothing. A floor can look gone and still be there. Trust is the mechanic.

SOUND

There are no music files. The browser builds the track. Each dimension has its own tempo and root note. As traps fire in a room, the music gets denser. Mute it from the HUD if the room is already loud enough.
