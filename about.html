NULLSCAPE

Dimension 1 is handmade. I placed every trap. Sixteen rooms. Last Laugh is the sixteenth, and it is a room again. Boss stage 1 is the seventeenth. Flat floor. 20 traps. The door waits until you walk up, then it moves 10 times. Later worlds come from a seed. Same seed, same pain. These rooms I made angry.

TWENTY WORLDS

I made the last room of every world a boss because I got tired of the stairs. The floor is flat. The door just sits there until you walk up, and if you stall for 30 seconds the boss gets bored and yeets it somewhere you aren't. Null throws the door 7 times. Ashwell starts at 9 and then it goes up by 2 every world. I had a spreadsheet. I lost the spreadsheet.

Null's fight is the long one. Everybody else fights like their world. They get their own moves, then a second set, then a last set that is actually different attacks. A mark shows up before the shot so you get a beat. If the shot gets you, that boss talks in their own color. Die to a normal trap and you still get the old line. Boss rooms have their own drums under the song. Last Light is slower and heavier and I get a little nervous about it.

When the moves run out the face goes tired, they fall, and they blow up. The blast kills you. You can still grab the door on the way down if you're stubborn.

Traps start at 10. Each world adds the old climb plus 10 more. The boss room doubles that, triples it from world 8, quadruples it from 18. Rooms get 5 blocks wider every world because the later traps were piled on the old map and it looked dumb. From world 4 about a quarter of the rooms are shafts. You climb or you drop. I kept the steps clean. Traps on stairs felt cheap, so they wait on the flat. Weird movement shows up in world 2, the horror guys in 5, the really cruel traps in 8, and from world 10 those stop being rare. Two signature traps per world. The list and the caps are under Traps if you want the numbers.

THE MARKS

A room is a pile of tiles plus a list of traps. These are the tiles. Dust and a fake beam never tick the SURGE counter. I put them there so you'd flinch at nothing. Works on me too.

#Solid floor or wall. The rare honest one. I don't use it as much as I should.
=Looks like brick. Stand on it and it crumbles. I put these where your feet want to rest. Sorry.
*Painted brick. You walk through it and feel dumb. That's the whole trick. Through is this, mostly.
iSolid, and you can't see it. Fall In hides the door behind a few. I know. Evil.
^The spike is one of the most basic traps every created. Truely a work of art. These ones point up out of the floor. Touch them and you're dead.
vSame spike, hanging from the ceiling. Worse angle. You jump into these and I get to say I told you.
BBounce pad. Yeet's has opinions, and then gravity gets soft. My stomach drops every single time.
!Teeth hiding in the floor. Tiny glint if you're lucky. Most people aren't. I'm most people.
oSaw. It sits, or it shows up late, or it decides it likes you. I gave it a personality. Mistake.
CA shard. In Shiny and Last Laugh they blow up. I still grab them. I'm weak.
EThe real exit. Gold roof. Touch it, leave, feel relief for about two seconds.
FA door that isn't the exit. Duller gold. Traps wait for you to touch it. I wait with them.

SCALING

  • Rooms15 + D World 1 is 16 handmade rooms plus boss stage 1. After that it's roomCount(d) = 15 + d. World 20 is 35. I counted.
  • Width+5 / world stageWidth = 32 + 5×(D−1). The floors stretch so the later traps aren't glued to that old 32-wide map.
  • ShaftsD4+ · ~1/4 About a quarter of the rooms. You go up or down eight or ten tiles. Saws and beams stay off the steps because that felt cheap.
  • Trap budget10 + climb Starts at 10. Each world adds (2 or 3) + 10. Boss room is ×2, ×3 from world 8, ×4 from world 18. The live numbers are on the world cards up there.
  • Activations+10 / world activationCap = 4 + 10×(D−1). How many times a trap that fires more than once can still bite you.
  • RareD2+ One weird movement trick a room. D10+ up to 2. The cap is in worlds.js if you go looking. I do.
  • EntityD5+ One horror thing. D10+ up to 2. Fog wants world 9. The hunt light wants world 10.
  • ApexD8+ sparse, about 1 room in 8. D10+ common. tier = 1 + floor((D−10)/3), sometimes one less. Harder numbers from world 15. I kept turning the knob.
  • QTED3+ about 1 room in 10. Presses go 1 to 6. The window shrinks about 0.045s a world and it won't go under 0.28s. Miss it and you die.
  • Signatures2 / world Stamped first, so that world has a lie that's only its. The family still uses the normal placement rules. I'm not reinventing holes.

  • JumpYou leave the ground near the start. Hope is a trigger. I know.
  • GroundIce only bites while your feet are down. In the air you're fine, which feels wrong.

QTE

World 3 and up, about 1 room in 10. The key pool grows with the world. Miss it and you die. Window = max(0.28, 1.15 − 0.045×(D−1)). Presses: world 3 is 1, 4 is 2, 8 is 3, 12 is 4, 16 is 5, 19 is 6. My hands hate 19.
