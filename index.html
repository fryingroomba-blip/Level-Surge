WALK THE LESSON

HOW A RUN WORKS

The floor is the teacher.

Scroll through the rooms. A shard flares when your hand sits on it. Each platform is a rule I will use on you.

The job

Touch the door with the gold roof. That room is over. Everything else is me trying to stop that.

  • A death puts you back in the same room. The skull count stays for the whole world.
  • Clear the last room and the next dimension opens. You walk them in order.
  • This browser remembers the world, the room, the deaths, and the time. Wipe the site data and I forget you.

Two doors

They are the same shape on purpose. The roof is the tell.

  • Real: bright gold roof, red door. That one ends the room.
  • Fake: duller gold. Knock Knock puts it on the right, because that is where people run.
  • Touching the fake one can spring the room. Psych makes you walk all the way back to the real one.
  • The boss door opens after his moves are spent.

What a trap waits for

A trap is a watch, then an answer. Sometimes the answer is late, so you think you are safe.

  • Walk: you pass a column. This is most of them.
  • Jump: you leave the ground near the spawn. Hope is a trigger.
  • Still: Don't Stop. Stand for 0.4 seconds and the floor drops.
  • Time: Wait For It holds the ceiling, then bricks you at 1.9 seconds.
  • Been past: the trap arms after you clear a mark. It fires on the way back.
  • Most fire once. A few arm again after a pause.

What it does to you

Dust and a glow are whispers. The trap count waits for a real hit.

  • Gate, hole, side teeth, ceiling teeth, and a falling brick take the floor or the air.
  • Saws chase. Lasers cross the lane. A push, a lock, wind, reverse, and gravity take the controls.
  • The trap count in the bar stays ???? until a real one fires. A tell leaves it alone.

The bar

Left to right, that row tells the run. It lies until a trap is real.

  • Dimension, room number, name, and pips. The lit pip is where you are. Red pips are rooms you already lived through.
  • SURGE stacks when you survive real traps inside 2.4 seconds. A death clears it. At ten it prints a joke number.
  • The diamond is shards from coins. In Shiny and Last Laugh those coins blow up. Everywhere else they are safe.
  • A cleared world scores 79,437,924 × (90 − deaths) × (260 − seconds). Each factor stays at least 1.

Hands

The keyboard and the phone buttons are the same jobs. I was stubborn about one of them.

  • Move with arrows or A and D. Jump with Space, W, or Up.
  • Dash is Shift. It arrives in Frostbite, world 3. People got stuck on ledges. That was me.
  • Dash keeps its speed and fires once per press. Shift plus A or D keeps the walk clean.
  • R retries the room. Esc pauses. The speaker mutes.

World 1, by hand

Sixteen rooms, then the boss. I placed every trap. Learn these and the later seeds make more sense.

  • Trust Me: dust, then the floor leaves.
  • Keep Jumping: the landing is already a hole.
  • Knock Knock: the right door is fake.
  • Wait For It: ceiling spikes, floor returns at 1.9 seconds.
  • Shiny: three coins over spikes. They explode.
  • Backwards: left and right swap.
  • Checkered: some bricks are only paint.
  • Buzz: the saw by the door starts chasing.
  • Yeet: the pad throws you, then gravity gets light.
  • Up Is Down: the door is on the ceiling.
  • Come Back: the door slides away while you walk.
  • Fall In: the door is down in the hole.
  • Don't Stop: 0.4 seconds of standing drops the floor.
  • Psych: fake door, then the long walk back.
  • Through: the middle wall is paint.
  • Last Laugh: the coins still explode, the right door is fake, and the real one is waiting when you return.

How a world grows

Same seed, same pain. Clear one dimension and the next one wakes up.

  • Seeded worlds have 15 rooms plus the dimension number. World 1 is the exception: 16 handmade rooms, then boss stage 1. World 20 has 35 rooms.
  • Floors widen by 5 blocks every world. Later traps get the extra room.
  • From world 4, about a quarter of the rooms are shafts. You climb to the door, or you drop to it. The steps stay clean. The traps wait on the flat.
  • The trap budget starts at 10. Each world adds the old climb plus 10 more. The boss room doubles that, triples it from world 8, and quadruples it from world 18.
  • Every world keeps two signature traps of its own. They are stamped first, so you can tell which world bit you.

The cruel tiers

They arrive on a schedule. Horror waits until world 5.

  • Rare movement tricks start in world 2. One per room, two from world 10. Blackout is world 3 and later, about one room in ten, and it holds in the dark.
  • Horror entities start in world 5. The fog needs world 9. The hunt light needs world 10.
  • Apex traps are sparse from world 8 and common from world 10. They get meaner again from world 15.
  • Quick-time presses start in world 3, about one room in ten. Miss the key, or wait too long, and you die. The window shrinks as the worlds go up. Presses go from 1 to 6.

The last room

The floor is flat. I got tired of the stairs. The boss is the room.

  • The door waits until you walk up to it. After 30 seconds the boss gets bored and drops it somewhere else.
  • Null keeps the long fight: his own look, his own music, and a separate cooldown on each heavy move. He throws the door 7 times.
  • From Ashwell on, the count starts at 9 and adds 2 each world. Those bosses fight like their world. Later phases bring new attacks.
  • A mark shows before a shot. If the shot gets you, the boss talks in its own color. Die to a room trap and you still get the old line.
  • When the moves are gone the face goes tired, it falls, and it blows up. The blast kills. After that, the door is open. Grab it on the way down.

What you hear

Each dimension has its own music engine. The about page keeps the old shared mix.

  • Boss rooms put their own drums under that world's song. The last world is slower and heavier.
  • Moving hazards get louder as they get closer.
  • Tap once if the browser is holding the sound. The speaker in the bar is the mute.
