TWENTY WORLDS

From world 2, the last room adds four traps and a fake door. Each world is 5 blocks wider than the one before it. I got bored of the floor just falling out, so each world keeps two lies the other worlds do not use.

Dash starts in world 3. About 1 room in 10 also asks you to hit a letter. Miss the letter and you die right then. World 3 wants one letter. World 19 wants six, and the timer is short. By world 5, a saw or something worse starts coming after you.
