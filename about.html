THE DEAL

Touch the real door and the room is cleared. That is the only honest rule.

  • Door E Gold roof, red door. The real one ends the room.
  • Fake F Same door. KNOCK KNOCK puts the joke on the right and the exit up on the left.
  • Rooms 15+D Nullscape has 16. Last Light has 35. Clear the set and the next world unlocks.
  • Death ☠ A roast, then the same room. The counter sticks until you start that world over.
  • Save v3 This browser, under the name level-surge-v3. Best deaths, best time, shards, how far you unlocked.

THE BAR

The top bar is the only narrator. It still lies about the trap count until something actually fires.

????

Stays a question until a trap fires. Dust and a glow are quiet. They do not tick it, and they do not build SURGE.

Room

Number, name, and pips. The lit pip is the room you are in. Red pips are rooms you already left.

SURGE

Traps you live through inside 2.4 seconds. At ten it prints SURGE x79437924. Die and the combo drops.

Shards

Gold coins. SHINY and LAST LAUGH detonate them. Every other room just counts them.

Time

How long this run of the world has lasted, and how many times the room has won.

Rating

On a full clear: 79,437,924 × (90 − deaths) × (260 − seconds), and neither factor goes under 1. Saved beside your best deaths and best time for that dimension.

NULLSCAPE

Dimension 1 is handmade. Sixteen rooms, ten traps each. Later worlds are built from a seed, so they come back the same, but they are not these rooms.

TWENTY WORLDS

From Dimension 2 on, the last room is a core: four extra traps, and a fake door. Trap count starts at 10 and climbs by 2, then 3, then 2, then 3. Each world also brings two signature trap types you will not see anywhere else.

THE MARKS

A room is a tile grid plus a list of traps. These are the tiles. A quiet trap, like dust or a fake beam, does not count toward SURGE.

#Solid floor or wall.
=Looks like brick. Crumbles when you stand on it.
*Painted brick. You pass through it.
iSolid, and invisible. FALL IN hides the door behind some.
^Spikes. Up from the floor.
vSpikes. Down from the ceiling.
BBounce pad. YEET's has opinions, and then gravity eases off.
!Hidden teeth in the floor. They show as a faint glint.
oSaw. It can sit still, arrive late, or decide it likes you.
CA shard. In SHINY and LAST LAUGH, greed is fatal.
EThe real exit.
FA door that is not the exit. Touching it is a condition traps wait for.

WHAT FIRES

A trap waits for something you do, then answers. A delay means it hits a beat late.

IT WAITS

  • WalkYou pass a column. Most rooms are built on this.
  • JumpToo soon, too early in the room, or after you have already jumped.
  • StillDON'T STOP drops the floor if you hesitate on it. The room itself crumbles at 0.4s of standing.
  • DoorThe fake one. PSYCH and LAST LAUGH start the long way back here.
  • TimeWAIT FOR IT holds the ceiling for 1.9 seconds, then the teeth in the floor become brick.

IT ANSWERS

  • HoleBrick gone, spikes under it. Sometimes the spikes pop back into floor.
  • TeethA row from the ceiling, or one spike under a door.
  • SawFrom offscreen, on a delay, or chasing once BUZZ wakes it.
  • BeamA real laser, or a glow that means nothing. The glow is quiet.
  • LieControls flip. A shove. The door moves. Gravity reverses. A brick falls.

SOUND

There are no music files. The browser builds the track.

  • Tempo BPM Each world has its own. Nullscape is 128. Iron Clock is 132. Last Light is 148. Hunger Moon is 108.
  • Root Hz The chord follows the world's root note. Nullscape sits at 92.5. The loop runs 96 bars.
  • Heat +trap As traps fire in the room, the arrangement gets thicker. Mute it from the speaker in the bar.
