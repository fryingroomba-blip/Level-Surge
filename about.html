THE DEAL

Real door = done. Everything else is the game being rude.

  • Door E Gold roof. Red door. This one is real. Congrats.
  • Fake F Same door. Wrong door. KNOCK KNOCK puts the joke on the right.
  • Rooms 15+D Nullscape: 16. Last Light: 35. Finish a world, unlock the next. Exciting.
  • Death ☠ You get roasted. Then you try again. The counter remembers. You will too.

Room

Name, number, little pips. Lit = where you are. Red = rooms you somehow survived.

SURGE

Traps you live through in 2.4 seconds. Hit ten and it prints SURGE x79437924. Die and it's gone. Classic.

Shards

Gold coins. SHINY and LAST LAUGH blow them up. Everywhere else they're just shiny. Weird.

Time

How long you've been in this world. And how many times the room won.

Rating

Clear a world: 79,437,924 × (90 − deaths) × (260 − seconds). Neither number goes under 1. We save your best humiliation.

NULLSCAPE

Dimension 1. Handmade. Sixteen rooms. Ten traps each. Later worlds are seeded. Same seed = same pain. Not these rooms though.

TWENTY WORLDS

From D2 on, last room is a core: four extra traps plus a fake door. Traps start at 10 and climb +2 / +3 forever. Each world also has two signature traps. Special little nightmares.

WHAT FIRES

A trap waits for you to do a thing. Then it does a worse thing. Sometimes late, for comedy.

IT WAITS

  • WalkYou pass a spot. Most of the game is this.
  • JumpToo soon. Too early. Or just because you jumped.
  • StillDON'T STOP drops the floor if you hesitate. 0.4s of standing = gone.
  • DoorThe fake one. PSYCH and LAST LAUGH start the long walk home here.
  • TimeWAIT FOR IT holds the ceiling 1.9s. Then the floor teeth turn into brick. Wow.

IT ANSWERS

  • HoleBrick gone. Spikes under. Sometimes they become floor again. Rude.
  • TeethCeiling row. Or one spike under a door. Classy.
  • SawOffscreen. Delayed. Or chasing once BUZZ wakes it.
  • BeamReal laser, or a glow that means nothing. Glow is quiet. Glow is a liar.
  • LieControls flip. Shove. Door moves. Gravity flips. Brick falls. Pick your poison.
